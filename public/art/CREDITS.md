# Art credits
All images in this folder were generated for Tremor with Higgsfield (model z_image) on 2026-10-30 from original text prompts, then chroma-keyed and downscaled by `tremor-art/scripts/process.py`. Nicoh checked Higgsfield's terms permit use. No id Software or Freedoom art is included.


Monster replacement sprites in public/wad/tremor.wad are palette transforms of
bundled Freedoom Phase 1 sprites, generated by scripts/build-tremor-wad.py. They
retain Freedoom's license: see ../wad/FREEDOOM-LICENSE.txt. The CREW sprites remain
original Tremor art. Procedural horror audio and Halloran text are original.
