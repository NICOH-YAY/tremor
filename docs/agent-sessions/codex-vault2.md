CORE FEEL (most important — make aiming effortless):

- Plant: hold Left Mouse. If the crosshair ray hits a surface within pole reach (e.g., 4.5 m), the pole tip sticks to that point (show a small highlight dot on surfaces in reach so the player knows before clicking; out-of-reach = no dot).
- Bend: while holding, the pole flexes (visual curve) and a bend amount grows as you keep holding / as you push toward the plant point; you can still look around to choose the launch direction. Release: you are launched from the plant point AWAY from it, roughly along the direction from the plant point to your camera, plus a bit of the camera's look direction, with speed proportional to bend (cap). - Mid-air re-plant is allowed if a surface is in reach (chaining vaults is the skill expression). WASD gives only very weak air/ground nudge (like 5–10% of a vault) so the pole is the only real locomotion.
